# Discipline Checklist for Low-Motivation Days
{: .fs-9 }

When motivation disappears, this is the minimum standard.
{: .fs-5 .fw-300 }

---

## Why This Exists

The hard part is rarely knowledge. It is execution when you are tired, distracted, or emotionally flat.

This checklist turns a high-intensity mindset into repeatable behavior.

---

## Non-Negotiables

- **One hard task first** before reactive work.
- **One promise kept** that you made to yourself.
- **One deliberate discomfort** (training, deep work, cleanup, outreach).
- **One distraction removed** from your environment.

Do these four, and the day remains aligned.

---

## The 30-Minute Reset Protocol

If you feel yourself slipping:

1. 2 minutes: Write the single most important task.
2. 20 minutes: Work in full focus, no switching.
3. 5 minutes: Close loops (notes, next step, schedule).
4. 3 minutes: Remove one friction source for tomorrow.

Repeat once if needed.

---

## Identity Statements (Use Sparingly, Mean Them)

- I keep my word when no one is watching.
- I do hard things without needing applause.
- I trade short-term relief for long-term respect.
- I raise my standard in inches, daily.

---

## Bottom Line

You do not need a perfect day. You need a credible one. Keep the standard, protect attention, and finish what you said you would do.
