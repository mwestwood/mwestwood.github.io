# Sleep — The Foundation of Everything
{: .fs-9 }

If you could only optimize one thing for your health, the research overwhelmingly points to sleep.
{: .fs-5 .fw-300 }

---

## Why Sleep Gets Ignored

Most people treat sleep as the thing they sacrifice to get more done. Late nights, early alarms, weekend "catch-up" — it feels productive.

The evidence says otherwise.

{: .important }
> Sleeping fewer than 7 hours is associated with increased risk of obesity, type 2 diabetes, cardiovascular disease, and impaired immune function. Matthew Walker, *Why We Sleep* (2017).

---

## What's Actually Happening While You Sleep

Sleep isn't one thing. It cycles through distinct stages roughly every 90 minutes:

- **NREM Stage 1 & 2** — Light sleep. Body temperature drops, heart rate slows. Memory consolidation begins.
- **NREM Stage 3 (Deep/Slow-wave sleep)** — Physical restoration. Growth hormone released. Immune system activated.
- **REM sleep** — Brain highly active. Emotional processing. Creative connections formed. Dreaming.

Early in the night, sleep is weighted toward **deep NREM**. In the second half, toward **REM**. Cutting sleep short cuts off your REM disproportionately.

## Practical Foundations

### Consistency matters more than duration

Going to bed and waking at the same time every day — including weekends — anchors your **circadian rhythm**. Irregular schedules fragment sleep quality even if total hours are the same.

### Light is the biggest lever

- **Morning:** Get bright (ideally sunlight) exposure within 30–60 minutes of waking. This sets your circadian clock and improves nighttime sleep quality.
- **Evening:** Dim warm lights 1–2 hours before bed. Blue-spectrum light delays melatonin release by 1–3 hours.

### Temperature

Core body temperature needs to *drop* ~1–2°C to initiate sleep. A cool room (around 65–68°F / 18–20°C) accelerates this.

### Caffeine has a long tail

Caffeine has a **half-life of ~5–7 hours**. A coffee at 2pm still has ~25% active in your system at midnight. Most sleep researchers suggest cutting off caffeine by early afternoon.

---

## A Simple Sleep Checklist

- [ ] Same wake time every day (including weekends)
- [ ] Morning sunlight within 1 hour of waking
- [ ] No caffeine after 1pm
- [ ] Dim lights after 9pm
- [ ] Cool bedroom (65–68°F)
- [ ] Phone out of the bedroom (or on Do Not Disturb)
- [ ] 7–9 hours in bed
