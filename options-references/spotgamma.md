# SpotGamma
{: .no_toc }

A market structure analytics platform focused on how options dealer positioning — particularly gamma exposure (GEX) — may influence price behavior in major indices and individual stocks. Known for its "key levels" framework and HIRO real-time indicator.

## What It Is

SpotGamma is built around a specific thesis: that the options market is large enough that the hedging activity of market makers (dealers) can influence underlying price movement — particularly in highly liquid index products. Its tools are designed to help traders identify where these hedging flows may create support, resistance, or volatility amplification zones.

Rather than a traditional flow alert tool ("someone just bought 5,000 calls"), SpotGamma asks a different question: "Given current dealer positioning, where might buying or selling pressure emerge from their hedging activity?"

---

## What It's Known For

- **Gamma Exposure (GEX)** — a model-based estimate of how much dealers must buy or sell shares to hedge their options books at various price levels
- **Key levels** — specific price levels derived from options positioning where dealer hedging activity may cluster
- **HIRO** — a real-time indicator attempting to capture near-term hedging flow impact on underlying price
- **FlowPatrol** — daily unusual options flow insights published as a daily report
- **Volatility dashboard** — IV and positioning data across strikes and expiries
- **Options calculator** — position modeling tool
- **Equity Hub** — extends the GEX framework to 3,500+ individual U.S. stocks
- **Active Discord + Q&A cadence** — twice-weekly live Q&A sessions with the SpotGamma team

## Best For

- Index and S&P 500 focused traders who want to incorporate dealer positioning context into their analysis
- Traders who want to understand *why* certain price levels may matter, not just *that* flow happened there
- Options traders who want volatility context around key strikes and expiries

---

## Strengths

- Unique and coherent "market structure" analytical lens not widely replicated at this price
- HIRO provides a real-time input to the framework, not just end-of-day data
- Active education through Discord Q&As helps users actually apply the framework
- GEX levels have become a widely referenced reference point in the broader retail options community

## Weaknesses / Risks

- **Model dependence** — GEX and dealer positioning estimates are model-based; different vendors use different assumptions and can produce different conclusions
- The framework works best in high-liquidity index options; may be less reliable for individual stocks
- Interpretation requires learning the SpotGamma framework — it is not plug-and-play
- Higher Alpha tier pricing is significant for retail traders
