## What It Is

thinkorswim is the advanced trading platform included with a Charles Schwab brokerage account. It is one of the most comprehensive options analysis and execution environments available at zero additional cost to retail traders. Its combination of live analytics, a historical backtester (thinkBack), and a full-featured simulator (paperMoney) makes it a default starting point for many options traders.

---

## What It's Known For

- **thinkBack** — options backtesting tool storing nearly a decade of historical options trade data; reconstruct historical positions and analyze how they would have performed
- **paperMoney** — simulated trading using live market data but virtual money; widely used for strategy testing and education
- **Options analytics** — live Greeks, P&L diagrams, theoretical prices, probability cones, and risk profiles on any position or strategy
- **Option chains** — detailed chains with filtering, sorting, and customizable Greek columns
- **Desktop platform** — the flagship multi-panel, highly configurable trading environment
- **Web and mobile** — modern web interface and mobile apps for monitoring and trading on the go
- **Education** — Schwab's educational content covers platform tutorials to options strategy courses

## Best For

- Active retail traders who want a comprehensive, all-in-one analysis and execution platform at no additional cost
- Options traders who want to backtest ideas without a separate subscription
- New options traders who want to paper trade before committing real capital

---

## Strengths

- Best value proposition in this comparison: professional-grade tools at zero platform cost for Schwab clients
- thinkBack is a genuinely useful backtesting tool — few brokerages include one at any price
- paperMoney with live data is the best free simulator in retail options
- Multi-platform coverage — desktop, web, and mobile — means you're never locked to one device
- Schwab's educational resources are among the best in retail brokerage

## Weaknesses / Risks

- Differences between simulated (paperMoney) and live execution — fills, slippage, and order routing differ
- Commission accumulation on high-frequency multi-leg strategies adds up at $0.65/contract/leg
- API integration is more limited compared to IBKR or Tradier — less suitable for automated strategies
- Schwab ecosystem lock-in: the best experience requires keeping assets at Schwab
