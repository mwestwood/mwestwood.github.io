# Unusual Whales
{: .no_toc }

A broad retail platform centered on options flow and alerts — one of the most recognized names in the retail options community, known for its tiered plan structure, mobile app, and strong community channels.

## What It Is

Unusual Whales aggregates completed U.S. options trades across all exchanges and surfaces them as a filterable, alertable flow feed. It is built primarily for retail traders who want to monitor large or unusual options activity without the infrastructure costs of professional data terminals.

The platform is transparent about how it works: it warns users that alerts are informational (not trade recommendations), that multi-leg trades and hedges cannot always be distinguished from directional bets, and that delays vary by plan.

---

## What It's Known For

- **Live options flow feed** with filtering by ticker, premium, expiry, size, and more
- **Tiered delay structure** — free users see alerts delayed 5–15 minutes; premium plans offer live (zero-delay) data
- **Alert distribution** across web, mobile app, and community channels
- **Community integration** — one of the most active retail options Discord communities
- **Education about flow interpretation** — documentation explicitly explains the bid/ask side heuristic and its limitations
- **Dark pool feed** alongside options flow

## Data & Coverage

| Field | Details |
|:---|:---|
| Data source | OPRA (consolidated U.S. options trades/quotes) |
| Update frequency | Plan-dependent: 5–15 min delay (free), live (premium) |
| Alert window | Typically 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM ET |
| Markets | U.S. equity and ETF options; dark pool prints |
| Open interest | Updated once daily (standard industry practice) |

## Platforms

- **Web** — primary interface with filterable flow table
- **Mobile app** — alert delivery and flow browsing
- **API** — available for power users; redistribution constraints documented in FAQ
- **Community** — Discord-based channels for linked account holders

---

## Best For

- Retail and active traders who want to monitor large options prints
- Traders looking for an entry-level flow tool with a free tier
- Community-oriented traders who learn from shared flow discussions

---

## Strengths

- Clear delay disclosures — you always know what tier of latency you're getting
- One of the largest retail options communities
- Explicit education about what flow signals can and cannot tell you
- Mobile alerts make it usable away from a desktop

## Weaknesses / Risks

- Flow misinterpretation risk: hedges and multi-leg trades look identical to directional prints
- Free tier delay (5–15 min) makes it unsuitable for true real-time intraday trading
- Flow data is not exportable (per their FAQ as of research date)
- API redistribution is restricted
