## What It Is

OptionMetrics occupies a different tier from the other platforms in this guide. It is not a retail flow tool, a strategy builder, or a real-time screener. It is an institutional historical data vendor whose IvyDB product has been the de facto standard in academic finance research for options pricing, implied volatility, and Greeks going back to January 1996.

Its primary customers are universities, hedge funds, risk desks, and quants who need clean, methodologically rigorous, long-history options data for backtesting, research, and model development.

---

## What It's Known For

- **IvyDB U.S.** — comprehensive U.S. equity, index, ETF, and ADR options data from January 1996 to present
- **Computed implied volatilities and Greeks** — pre-calculated for every contract using standardized methodology
- **Standardized constant-maturity volatility surfaces** — constructed for consistent cross-sectional and time-series research
- **Daily correction patches** — errors are corrected via a daily patch file, maintaining data integrity over time
- **Permanent identifiers** — corporate actions and security-level continuity handled through permanent IDs
- **Global coverage** — IvyDB extends to Europe, Asia-Pacific, Canada, futures options, and specialized datasets
- **Institutional support model** — dedicated support with documented business-hours coverage and 24×7 for urgent issues

## Strengths

- Longest clean history of any dataset in this comparison (1996 to present)
- Daily patch files maintain data integrity — errors are corrected, not just overwritten
- Standardized methodology makes it the most comparable dataset across research studies
- Institutional-grade support with explicit SLA documentation
- Global coverage extends far beyond U.S. equity options

## Weaknesses / Risks

- Not accessible to individual retail traders — enterprise pricing and sales model
- End-of-day data only — no intraday or real-time product
- If you backtest with OptionMetrics' IV/Greeks methodology then trade with a different vendor's Greeks, model mismatch risk is real
- Cost and access complexity are significant barriers
