{: .no_toc }

## What It Is

Cheddar Flow is a web-based options flow and alerts platform focused on surfacing unusual options activity for retail traders. It markets AI-driven alerting as one of its differentiators, aiming to help traders cut through noise in the daily options tape.

{: .note }
> Cheddar Flow's site has restricted automated access for research tools. Details here are based on publicly indexed content — verify specifics directly on their platform.

---

## What It's Known For

- **Unusual options flow alerts** — surfacing large or anomalous options prints in real time (or near-real-time)
- **AI-driven alerting** — marketed as a smarter filter layer on top of raw flow data
- **Institutional-style framing** — the platform positions its flow intelligence as the kind of signal typically reserved for professional desks
- **Retail-accessible pricing** — multiple plan options aimed at individual traders

## Strengths

- Positioned around actionable flow signals rather than raw data dumps
- AI-driven filtering aims to reduce noise compared to unfiltered tape
- Multiple plan options to match different budget levels

## Weaknesses / Risks

- **Same flow interpretation risk as all UOA tools** — hedges and multi-leg trades are indistinguishable from directional prints
- Limited public transparency about exact data sources and latency (verify directly)
- No confirmed API or data export options in publicly available information
