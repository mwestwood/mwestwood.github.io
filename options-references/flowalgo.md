# FlowAlgo
{: .no_toc }

A web-based options flow platform that sets itself apart with unusually honest disclosures about real-world data latency and dark pool reporting mechanics — positioning itself as a data provider rather than a stock picks service.

## What It Is

FlowAlgo streams real-time options order flow, equity blocks, and dark pool prints to its subscribers. What distinguishes it from competitors is its candor: it explicitly documents that "real-time" data can arrive 500ms to ~15 seconds late depending on system conditions, and that dark pool prints are often delayed hours or until the next trading day — not as a failure of its system, but as a structural reality of how dark pool reporting works under exchange rules.

---

## What It's Known For

- **Real-time options order flow** with filtering and alert capability
- **Dark pool prints and equity block data** — one of the few consumer platforms that surfaces these together with options flow
- **Historical flow lookback** — search prior flow data for specific tickers or conditions
- **Voice alerts** — audible notifications for incoming flow signals
- **"Institutional zones/levels"** — areas of significant historical price activity
- **Transparency about latency** — detailed disclosures about practical data delays that most platforms omit

## Data & Coverage

| Field | Details |
|:---|:---|
| Data source | OPRA-based options flow + dark pool equity prints |
| Options flow delay | ~500ms–15 seconds under normal conditions (explicitly disclosed) |
| Dark pool delay | Can be hours late or next-day due to exchange reporting rules |
| Equity block delay | Up to 20 minutes (per exchange rules, explicitly disclosed) |
| Markets | U.S. equities options + dark pool equity prints |

{: .highlight }
> FlowAlgo's explicit latency disclosures are rare in this industry. Most platforms simply say "real-time" without explaining the practical nuances. Reading their disclosure page is a useful exercise even if you use a different platform.

## Best For

- Active intraday retail traders who want options flow and dark pool data together
- Traders who value platform honesty about data limitations over marketing claims
- Those running both options flow and equity block/dark pool strategies

---

## Strengths

- Best-in-class transparency about what "real-time" actually means in practice
- Combines options flow and dark pool prints in one interface
- Historical lookback for researching past flow on specific names
- Voice alerts for hands-free monitoring

## Weaknesses / Risks

- Dark pool data is often not truly real-time — structural delay is unavoidable
- No broker integration for direct execution from the platform
- Dark pool prints don't directly indicate buy vs sell direction
- Web-only; no mobile app confirmed
