{: .no_toc }

## What It Is

dxFeed is not a retail trader platform. It is an enterprise market-data infrastructure provider that offers an unusual options screener positioned for organizations — allowing brokerages, fintechs, and data distributors to embed unusual options activity capabilities into their own products without building the underlying data pipeline themselves.

---

## What It's Known For

- **Configurable UOA screening** — the unusual options screener can be parameterized to match an organization's specific definition of "unusual activity"
- **Data curation handled internally** — multi-leg trade normalization, OPRA processing, and data issues are handled at the infrastructure level, not by the client
- **B2B and enterprise positioning** — designed for organizations serving their own end users, not for individual traders
- **OPRA-aligned data infrastructure** — built on top of consolidated U.S. options market data

## Best For

- Fintechs that want to add unusual options flow to their platform without building data infrastructure

---

## Strengths

- Data curation and OPRA processing handled at the infrastructure level — reduces engineering burden for client organizations
- Configurable screening means each organization can define "unusual" to match their specific use case
- Scalable infrastructure suited to organizational rather than individual consumption

## Weaknesses / Risks

- Not designed for individual retail traders — no retail interface or consumer pricing
- Enterprise pricing and sales cycle are barriers compared to consumer-priced tools
- Downstream clients who misinterpret UOA as directional certainty face the same flow interpretation risks as any other platform
- Limited public documentation makes pre-sales technical evaluation difficult
