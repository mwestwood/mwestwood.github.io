# Barchart
{: .no_toc }

A broad market-data portal with options screeners, unusual options activity lists, and one of the most transparent data delay disclosures in the retail space — a practical scanner hub for broad market research rather than a dedicated real-time flow engine.

## What It Is

Barchart is a comprehensive market data portal covering stocks, options, futures, and forex. Its unusual options activity (UOA) pages and options screeners are widely used by retail traders as a free or low-cost entry point for scanning unusual options prints. It covers far more asset classes than options-specific platforms — making it a useful broad research hub even if it lacks the depth of single-purpose options tools.

---

## What It's Known For

- **Unusual options activity screener** — one of the most commonly referenced free UOA lists in retail trading
- **Options screener** with extensive filter options
- **Explicit data delay disclosure** — unusually transparent about exactly when data updates and how delayed it is
- **Email digests** — daily and periodic newsletters highlighting unusual options activity
- **Broad market coverage** — stocks, futures, forex, and more alongside options
- **Fundamental data integration** — fundamental data sourced from Zacks and Morningstar

---

## Data & Coverage

| Field | Details |
|:---|:---|
| Options screener start | Updates begin ~9:50 AM ET |
| Options data delay | ~25–30 minutes |
| Update frequency | Every ~5 minutes after initial load |
| Markets | U.S. options (and Canadian options with different thresholds); futures; forex |
| Fundamental data | Sourced from Zacks and Morningstar |

{: .highlight }
> Barchart's transparency about its data delay is a model other platforms should follow. The 25–30 minute delay makes it unsuitable for real-time flow chasing but excellent for end-of-day research and scanning for persistent unusual activity.

---

## Pricing

Many advanced features — historical UOA reports, deeper filtering, additional screener parameters — are gated behind paid Premier memberships. Core UOA pages are accessible with a free account.

---

## Platforms

- **Web** — primary interface; screeners and options chain viewer
- **Email** — newsletters and digest delivery for unusual options activity

---

## Best For

- Retail traders who want a free or low-cost starting point for unusual options activity scanning
- Traders doing end-of-day or longer-timeframe research (not intraday flow chasing)
- Those who want broad market context — stocks, options, futures — in one destination

---

## Strengths

- Free UOA access makes it one of the most accessible starting points for retail traders
- Transparent delay disclosure prevents false confidence in data freshness
- Broad asset class coverage beyond just options
- Email digest workflow is useful for staying informed without active monitoring

## Weaknesses / Risks

- 25–30 minute delay makes it unsuitable for intraday real-time flow trading
- Bullish/bearish labels are based on bid/ask heuristics — same interpretation risk as all UOA tools
- Less analytical depth than specialized platforms — no IV surface, no Greeks, no strategy builder
