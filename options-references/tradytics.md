# Tradytics
{: .no_toc }

## What It Is

Tradytics packages a wide collection of market tools into a single subscription, making it one of the most feature-dense platforms in the retail options space. Its distinguishing trait is breadth: instead of excelling at one thing, Tradytics covers flow, dark pools, dealer positioning, news, charting, and automated Discord alerts under one roof.

---

## What It's Known For

- **Wide tool collection** — options dashboard, dark pools, charts, sentiment, and news summarizer
- **Dealer positioning analytics** — delta, gamma, vanna, and charm flow analysis built into the platform
- **Discord bot integration** — bots that auto-post scans, alerts, and flow data into any server; webhooks supported
- **Mobile apps** — iOS and Android versions (described as lightweight versions of the web platform)
- **Multi-asset reach** — Discord bot page references crypto scans, indicating coverage beyond U.S. equities

## Platforms

---

## Best For

- Active retail traders who live in Discord and want their tools to come to them
- Traders who want a single subscription covering flow, dark pools, and dealer positioning
- Semi-systematic users who want dashboards rather than code-based analysis

---

## Strengths

- Broadest feature set per dollar in the retail options space
- Discord automation is a genuine workflow differentiator for community-based traders
- Free tier lets you evaluate before committing to a subscription
- Multi-platform coverage — web, iOS, Android, and Discord

## Weaknesses / Risks

- **Tool sprawl** — access to many tools can lead to signal overload rather than clarity
- Dealer positioning models involve assumptions that differ by vendor — understand the methodology before relying on it
- Mobile apps are described as lightweight versions of the full web experience
- Discord bot pricing is separate and additional
