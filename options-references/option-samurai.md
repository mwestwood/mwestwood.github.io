## What It Is

Option Samurai flips the typical options research workflow. Instead of starting with unusual flow and asking "what does this mean?", it starts with your strategy criteria and asks "which contracts match this today?" You define what you want — a covered call with a specific yield, a short put with a specific IV rank, an iron condor with defined risk-reward — and Option Samurai surfaces every contract in the market that fits.

---

## What It's Known For

- **Custom strategy scanner** — scan for specific options strategies using rule-based criteria
- **170+ filters** — IV rank, IV vs realized volatility, delta, days to expiration, yield, bid/ask spread, and more
- **Scan watching and notifications** — set scans to run intraday and receive alerts when new matches appear
- **Volatility suite** — IV rank, IV percentile, implied vs realized volatility comparisons
- **Trade log and journal** — track and review past trades within the platform
- **Spreadsheet toolbox** — Excel and Google Sheets integration for working with scan results
- **Broker transmission** — direct trade submission to select brokers (additional brokers noted as coming soon)
- **Mobile version** — full-featured mobile access claimed for all platform features

## Best For

## Strengths

- "Scan for strategy" workflow is unique — most platforms show what happened; Option Samurai shows what you could do today
- 170+ filters provide genuine flexibility for systematic scanners
- Spreadsheet integration allows export and further analysis in familiar tools
- 14-day free trial available before committing

## Weaknesses / Risks

- Scanner outputs still require execution-quality checks — liquidity, bid/ask spread, and real-world fill quality matter
- Data source details are not enumerated — verify the specific feeds if your workflow depends on data provenance
- Best suited for options sellers and defined-risk strategies; less useful for flow-chasing traders
- Broker transmission coverage is limited; more brokers noted as coming soon
