## What It Is

LiveVol Pro and LiveVol Core are Cboe's professional analytics platforms, sitting at the institutional end of the options analytics spectrum. Where retail tools surface filtered flow alerts, LiveVol exposes the raw tape — tick-level time and sales for every options print going back to 2011. Combined with streaming data, advanced scanners, skew analysis, earnings tools, and real-time Excel RTD, it is built for professionals who need to interrogate the options market at depth.

---

## What It's Known For

- **Tick-level time and sales from 2011 to present** — one of the deepest historical options tape archives available to non-institutional users
- **Streaming real-time trade data** — live tape feed for active monitoring
- **Excel RTD integration** — pull live options data directly into spreadsheets via Real-Time Data functions
- **Earnings analysis tools** — analyze implied move vs realized move and historical earnings patterns
- **Advanced scanners** — filter the entire options market by Greeks, IV, volume, and more
- **Skew charts** — visualize the implied volatility surface across strikes and expiries
- **Cboe DataShop ecosystem** — access to additional data products and exchange market data bundles

## Best For

- Active professionals and institutional desks who need deep tape access and long history
- Advanced retail traders willing to pay for institutional-quality historical data

---

## Strengths

- Deepest historical time and sales archive in the non-institutional retail-accessible market
- Excel RTD is a genuine workflow differentiator for spreadsheet-heavy analysts
- Cboe backing means reliable infrastructure and strong data quality
- Earnings tools provide structured historical context for volatility trading

## Weaknesses / Risks

- **Pricing** — $350+/mo plus data fees is the highest in this comparison for retail-accessible tools
- **Market data fee complexity** — surcharges for additional feeds can make total cost unpredictable
- Professional licensing rules add administrative overhead for institutional users
- Designed for professionals — less hand-holding than consumer-focused platforms
